Subject: High-Value Promo Code Strategy for Q1 2026

Overview:
This document outlines our confidential strategy for deploying high-value promo codes to VIP customers. These codes link to significant discounts and are tied to financial incentives. Unauthorized exposure could lead to revenue loss or fraud.

Key Objectives:
- Increase customer retention by 25% through targeted promotions.
- Drive $5M in additional sales via affiliate partnerships.
- Minimize leakage by storing codes in secure buckets only.

Promo Code Deployment Plan:
1. Generate 10,000 unique codes in format WIZ-XXX-### (e.g., WIZ-VIP-001 for 50% off).
2. Segment by customer tier:
   - Tier 1 (VIP): Codes like WIZ-ELT-777 offering Buy1Get1 + Free Shipping.
   - Tier 2: Standard discounts, e.g., WIZ-STD-456 for 20%.
3. Integration: Link codes to eCommerce backend; track redemptions in real-time.

Risk Assessment:
- Storage: All codes must be in encrypted S3 buckets. Avoid public access.
- Monitoring: Use DSPM to scan for patterns like WIZ-[A-Z]{3}-[0-9]{3}.
- Contingency: If leaked, invalidate batch via API.

Budget Breakdown:
- Code Generation Tool: $10K
- Marketing Campaign: $50K
- Payouts: Estimated $200K based on 30% redemption rate.

Next Steps:
