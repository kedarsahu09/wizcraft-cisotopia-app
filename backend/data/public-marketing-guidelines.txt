Wiztopia Public Marketing Guidelines
Version: 2.1
Date: November 20, 2025
Sensitivity: Unclassified

Introduction:
These guidelines are for public use and outline best practices for promoting Wiztopia products. Share freely with partners and affiliates.

Core Principles:
- Focus on fun: Emphasize magical toys and gadgets for all ages.
- Transparency: Always disclose promo terms.
- Inclusivity: Appeal to diverse audiences.

Sample Promo Ideas:
- Holiday Special: Use codes like WIZ-HOL-123 for 10% off (publicly shareable).
- Social Media Tips: Post images of products with #WiztopiaMagic.

Do's and Don'ts:
- Do: Encourage user-generated content.
- Don't: Promise unapproved discounts.
